Who says you always need to hire an external venue for a top-tier company anniversary or summer party? Your own business premises are often an underrated gold mine: no room hire, no closing time, and full alignment with your brand.

But how do you turn a functional car park or a chilly warehouse into a place where your team actually wants to celebrate? Here are five creative ways we can transform your site.

## 1. Cocktail trailer and Cocktail Bamboo Bar

An empty car park can feel lost—we offer **two strong setups**: our **cocktail trailer** (fully weatherproof and self-contained) and the modular **Cocktail Bamboo Bar** for lounge-style presence on site. Depending on space and concept, we deploy either or both. Once the bar is live and the first round of shaking begins, the workday is forgotten—with real festival energy right outside your office door.

## FAQ: your on-site corporate celebration

### What needs to be prepared in terms of tech?

Usually just space and a standard power supply. Glassware, ice and poseur tables are typically brought by a mobile catering service. For higher power demand, heavy-duty power or distribution should be clarified in advance.

### Is cocktail catering worth it for small company anniversaries?

Yes. In smaller teams especially, a professional bar signals appreciation and creates a relaxed conversation you rarely get around a conference table.

### How does breakdown work?

A well-rehearsed team stows the equipment and glassware after the event; with reusable solutions, no packaging waste is left behind. It helps to agree on breakdown time and access in advance.
